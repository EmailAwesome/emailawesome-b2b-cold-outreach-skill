# B2B event invitation preparation

Use this recipe inside the B2B Cold Outreach skill for a new B2B event before the first invitation. Email Awesome verifies the authorized address list; the event organizer owns the invitation policy. This does not cover post-registration activation or event-platform operation.

1. Confirm date, time zone, format, location, actual capacity, organizer, approved agenda, speakers already confirmed and sponsor package facts. Missing facts stay unknown.
2. Separate speaker, sponsor and attendee audiences. Preserve source IDs, source permission, suppression and event relevance. A past attendance record is not permission for a new invitation.
3. Follow the parent skill's Email Awesome verification and identity reconciliation. VALID plus opt-out remains excluded; uncertainty stays on hold.
4. Draft a different value/ask per role: speaker availability and topic; sponsor fit and an approved proposal; attendee learning outcome and registration information. Do not promise audience size, sponsor benefits or speaker participation without evidence.
5. Deliver the contact ledger plus this event brief:

| Audience | Confirmed benefit | Proposed ask | Unknown facts | Draft owner | Approval state |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speaker | Topic and intended audience | Discuss availability | Participation unconfirmed | Program owner | Draft |
| Sponsor | Approved package only | Explore fit | Rates/benefits pending when absent | Partnerships owner | Hold if facts missing |
| Attendee | Approved session outcome | Review registration information | Speakers not confirmed | Event owner | Draft |

Use one relevant follow-up per audience. Keep all messages unsent. Sending, enrolling attendees, reserving inventory and promising sponsorship terms need their own user request and approval. The parent skill's output.csv remains the contact contract; keep event facts and audience roles in the accompanying brief.
